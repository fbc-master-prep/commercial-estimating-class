Save & sync

Keeping your work with the file

Your work is saved on this computer automatically. To carry it into Teams, use one of these two.
1 · Save a filled copy
Creates a new .html file with all of your work baked inside it. Upload that file to Teams — anyone who opens it sees your data. Works in every browser.
2 · Link to a data file
Point the app at a file in your synced Teams folder. Every change writes to it automatically and re-opening picks up where you left off. Chrome or Edge on desktop.
Send to facilitator

One file, everything in it

Exports your property record, all of your deliverables, your notes, and every document you attached — as a single .json file. Email it to your facilitator.
Large photo sets make large files. If your file is over about 20 MB, most email systems will reject it — use a shared drive link instead, or export without photos below.
